# Complete DE441 Moon coefficients

The foundation introduced in #278 preserves every derived DE441 coefficient. The [integration method](de441-full-integration-method.md) describes its production routing outside the unchanged DE440 window, retirement of the compact representation and current public qualification.

The official [DE441 kernel](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/ftp/eph/planets/bsp/de441.bsp) contains target 301 and target 399 positions relative to target 3. The generator reads matching four-day SPK type 2 records in frame 1 and validates every record midpoint and radius. The [NAIF SPK description](https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/req/spk.html#Type%202:%20Chebyshev%20(position%20only)) defines the position-polynomial layout and analytic velocity. DE441 coordinates are ICRF; the native EQJ adapter applies the existing frame-bias rotation.

Each stored coefficient is `binary64(moonCoefficientKm - earthCoefficientKm)`, in that order. All thirteen coefficients on each of three axes are retained. The 28,489,578 derived coefficients occupy 227,916,624 bytes. Their bits are preserved without degree truncation, Float32 conversion or quantization. These derived bits are not either original component array. Both original arrays are reproducible from the 189 SHA-256-pinned HTTP byte ranges in `de441-full-evidence.json`; the complete 3,307,878,400-byte kernel was not downloaded or assigned a whole-file digest. The HTTP size, ETag and Last-Modified identity are recorded separately from content hashes.

Every rounded subtraction is enclosed by one ulp of its finite binary64 result. On each record, summing those errors bounds position because each Chebyshev basis value is at most one. Weighting by `n²/2` bounds the derivative per TDB day on a four-day record. Outward-rounded sums and Euclidean norms give full-domain composition enclosures of 1.0807320292516933e-10 km and 2.6799686710431294e-11 km/TDB-day. These enclose composition of the original binary64 component polynomials; they do not establish floating-point evaluator error or physical accuracy.

The table covers `[-1461004.5, 1461003.5)` TDB days from J2000. An extra complete lower record gives room for the existing private two-day emission interval below the public −1,461,000 TT-day boundary. The upper source margin is 3.5 days beyond the public upper TT endpoint; the rational coefficient enclosure in `de441-full-component-evidence.json` bounds the absolute TT-to-TDB offset by 2.585096462158265e-8 days over the entire padded TT interval. The adapter uses the analytic TDB-days-per-TT-day multiplier for velocity. The original TDB value determines domain acceptance; a rounded offset cannot admit an exterior endpoint. Source and chunk seam tests check the exact value and both neighbouring binary64 times.

Ninety bundled `.bin` resources hold the 312-byte records in chunks of at most 8,192 records. The generated Swift index records each resource's exact byte count and SHA-256 digest. The shared loader maps a selected chunk read-only, verifies its size and digest before access, and reads little-endian binary64 values without changing their order or bits. SwiftPM copies the resource directory into the package bundle; missing, malformed, truncated, or corrupt resources fail closed, with no download or representation fallback.

Reproduce generation with `python3 Scripts/generate-moon-de441-full.py --cache <verified-range-directory>`. `--check` validates every embedded bit against the manifest, canonical source rendering and complete chunk inventory without a network request. `--verify-source` additionally re-reads the pinned component records and compares every independently recomputed subtraction with the stored bytes. Python controls cover malformed shape, alphabet, nonfinite source records, overflow, signed zero, subnormals and subtraction bounds. Native tests compare every decoded bit against Foundation's separate Base64 decoder and compare Clenshaw states against the retained 1,365 direct-source recurrence fixtures. Those fixtures are source-model checks, not new independent physical truth.

`qualify-moon-de441-full.py --check` requires the frozen nine-record inventory, each exact ordered Moon/Earth range pair, both pins, record index, byte offset and midpoint before repeating the 80-digit arithmetic at five phases. Its `--verify-source` mode independently reconstructs that sample recipe from the pinned SPK layout and verifies both retained component-record byte slices against their complete hashed HTTP ranges. Missing, extra, duplicate or mismatched samples and ranges are rejected; rebinding a modified coefficient’s ordinary digest cannot bypass the original-source comparison. Maximum sampled per-axis composition differences are 3.160748740412675e-11 km and 6.811792755202924e-12 km/TDB-day; they are arithmetic observations, not physical error limits.

`de441-full-storage-evidence.json` preserves the historical Base64 compiler and runtime observations at their original revisions. Its current representation section separately binds the generated index, all 90 mapped resources, shared loader, package manifest, generator, qualifier, runtime reader, and tests. `qualify-moon-de441-full.py --record-storage` performs this representation migration through verified descriptors and resources without relabelling or rerunning the historical workloads; `--check` recomputes the full inventory and rejects missing or changed inputs. The older 304,660,529-byte generated-source, 440,729,056-byte Release object, and 462,350,976-byte test executable observations describe the retired compiled-literal representation and remain historical context rather than measurements of the mapped representation.
