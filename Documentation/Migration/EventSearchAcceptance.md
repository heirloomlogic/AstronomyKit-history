## Limits

Publisher fixtures establish the stated finite samples and intervals. They do not certify continuous accuracy across the accepted ephemeris range. The complete-cycle census qualifies one source-selected nominal cycle for each outer planet; outside those selected spans the retained trajectory has no new event-time claim. The USNO archive exposes navigation output but not the rise/set service implementation. Its optical-limb evidence supports the selected convention without establishing the Sun's physical radius. Horizons and USNO future Earth-orientation values retain their documented prediction or model limits. Performance values depend on the recorded host and toolchain.
